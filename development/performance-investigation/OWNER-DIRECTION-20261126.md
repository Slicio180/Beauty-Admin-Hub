# Owner direction — 26 November 2026

Alessio clarified: Beauty is not moving to another registration platform. Research Jotform, Typeform, Tally, Ticket Tailor, Eventbrite and similar products to learn from their approaches and develop suitable ideas within Beauty. Product comparisons are learning material, not a migration proposal.

The performance goal serves both the player and organiser:
- Quickly and durably save registration requests.
- Promptly show saved players in the Hub with truthful processing status.
- Separately measure player receipt, Hub appearance and verified readiness for the draw.
- Improve actual completion time as well as visible responsiveness.
- Retain duplicate prevention, consent/message/source integrity and automatic recovery when clients close.
- Preserve the last good roster through connection/read failures.

External product documentation does not establish measured Beauty speed gains. Benchmark changes in isolated TEST; retain the accepted baseline and do not alter LIVE merely to investigate. No platform migration or infrastructure replacement is authorized by these research discussions.
